### 🔬 About me

For more than 20 years, I've worked in pharmaceutical and biopharmaceutical laboratories, where every result must be **traceable, reproducible and audit-ready**. I'm now completing a full-time Data Analyst bootcamp at **Jedha** to apply that same rigor to data.

- 🎓 MSc in Analytical Chemistry, University of Geneva
